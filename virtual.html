Virtual Pilot Platform

A Digital Twin for SMHPs

Overview

Credibility drives our hydrogen vision. Real-world pilots cost £5M and take 2 years, so our virtual pilot uses a modular digital twin to simulate second-life PV panels. It models DC/DC circuitry and PEM electrolysis under variable inputs, achieving 85% accuracy in energy output predictions, built with open-source tools.

Concepts

Open innovation via a plug-in architecture fosters disruptive value, inviting R&D partners like NREL to test innovations. Our design, though 10% less efficient initially, recovers 90% of waste heat, positioning it for physical pilot integration by mid-2026.

Build-out Plan

Leverages pvlib (BSD-3), ngspice (BSD-3), and NREL/electrolyzer (Apache-2.0) with Unity visuals to cut R&D costs by 30%. The FMI standard supports real-time monitoring, safety testing, and a scalable ecosystem, with a prototype ready by Q4 2025 at £200K.

What to Pick

Recommended: Open-source core (pvlib, ngspice) + FMI + Unity. Use bifacial_radiance for shading analysis and NREL/electrolyzer for efficiency modeling. This stack supports 1 MW simulations with 95% uptime, ideal for partner testing.

IP and Monetization

BSD/MIT/Apache licenses enable closed-source distribution. We plan per-seat licenses (£500/year) and a SaaS model (£100/month), generating £50K by 2027. FMI boundaries support partner plug-ins, with an SDK marketplace projected at £200K revenue by 2028.

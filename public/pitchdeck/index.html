01 — The Problem

The world is paying to switch clean power off.

When wind and solar generate more than the grid can carry, operators pay generators to stop. The clean electricity is simply discarded — and the cost is passed to consumers.

The bottleneck is transmission, not generation — and it is the same story on every fast-growing grid, from Europe to the US, China and Brazil.

03 — The Market

Clean hydrogen demand is global, mandated, and unmet.

The world already runs on hydrogen — almost entirely the dirty kind. Replacing it is a like-for-like market that exists today, before counting new demand in steel, shipping and transport. Roughly 50% of global food supply depends on nitrogen fertilisers derived from hydrogen — making green hydrogen a direct lever on food security.

Governments have set hard low-carbon targets and have no cheap way to supply them. The market exists; the affordable clean supply does not.

Our Mission

Fluxero builds Small Modular Hydrogen Plants (SMHPs) that reuse second-life PV panels to cut costs by 40% (£0.20/Wp saved) and generate H2 at £2/kg—replacing diesel gensets with zero-emission fuel cells for industrial backup and data centers. Our goal is to deploy 5 MW capacity by 2027, reducing CO2 emissions by 15,000 tonnes annually.

Why SMHPs?

Modular plants reuse e-waste PV to avoid 1M tonnes landfill/year, tolerate low-grade waste heat (60-80% efficiency), and stack for 1-10 MW output—no subsidies needed for base case viability. This delivers a 3-year ROI for industrial clients, targeting data centers and manufacturing hubs.

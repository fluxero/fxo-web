Fluxero Pilot

Tees Valley Innovation

Overview

Our 1 MW Tees Valley site, co-located with industrial operations, leverages second-life solar panels to feed variable-input electrolysers. We aim to produce 50 tonnes of green hydrogen annually, with fuel cells delivering 80% uptime for backup power. Deployment is slated for Q2 2026, with a £1.5M capex targeting a 3-year breakeven.

Progress & Partners

We’re 60% through site validation, with soil tests confirming stability for a 5-acre footprint. Partnerships with SolarCycle (panel recycling) and Siemens (electrolyser tech) are inked, aiming for 200 kW initial output by Q1 2026. Regulatory approval is on track, with a £300K grant application pending.
